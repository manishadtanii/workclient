Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you understand our US accounting services.

What accounting software do you work with?

We primarily work with QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop. We also support Xero, FreshBooks, and other popular US accounting platforms based on your business needs.

How do you handle payroll for multi-state employees?

We manage multi-state payroll with accurate state tax withholdings, SUTA/FUTA compliance, and timely filings for each state. We handle state registrations and ensure you remain compliant across all operating states.

Can you help with catch-up bookkeeping if my books are behind?

Absolutely! Our catch-up and clean-up service is specifically designed for businesses with backlogged or inaccurate books. We'll bring your financials current, correct errors, and establish proper processes going forward.

Do you coordinate with my CPA for tax filing?

Yes, we prepare tax-ready financials and work directly with your CPA during tax season. We provide all necessary schedules, adjustments, and supporting documentation to make the filing process seamless.

What's the difference between Controller and Virtual CFO support?

Controller services focus on day-to-day financial management, month-end close, and compliance oversight. Virtual CFO services provide strategic financial leadership including fundraising support, board-level reporting, KPI development, and growth planning.
