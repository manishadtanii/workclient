Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you understand our global accounting service.

Which countries do you support?

We specialize in India-US, India-UK, India-Middle East, and India-Southeast Asia corridors. For other regions, we have a network of international partners to ensure compliance and reporting.

How do you handle currency conversion?

We use live FX rates for accurate conversion, track FX gains and losses separately, and provide consolidated reporting in your base currency. We can also advise on hedging strategies to minimize currency risk.

What is transfer pricing compliance?

Transfer pricing ensures inter-company transactions between entities in different countries are priced at arm's length. We prepare required documentation, analysis, and filing to ensure compliance with local tax authorities.

Can you handle multiple currencies in real-time?

Yes, we integrate live currency feeds and provide real-time consolidated reporting across all operating currencies. All transactions are tracked in their original currency and converted for consolidation.

What's your turnaround time for international filings?

Our 24/7 global team ensures quick turnarounds. Most filing and compliance work is completed within 5-10 business days depending on jurisdiction-specific requirements and document availability.
