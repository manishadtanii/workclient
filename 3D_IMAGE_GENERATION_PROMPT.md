# 3D Image Generation Prompts for Eleware Accounting Website

## Website Theme & Design Analysis

### Brand Identity
- **Business**: Eleware Accounting - B2B Accounting, Tax, Compliance, and Advisory Services
- **Target Audience**: Startups, SMEs, E-commerce, Professional Firms across Delhi NCR
- **Brand Promise**: Financial clarity, compliance, growth, accuracy, transparency

### Color Palette
- **Primary Color**: #0B1F3A (Deep Navy Blue)
- **Secondary/Accent**: #42BDBD (Teal/Turquoise)
- **Tertiary**: #2DAAAA (Darker Teal for hover states)
- **Neutral**: White (#fff), Off-white (rgba(255,255,255,0.7))
- **Text**: Muted blue-gray (#6B7A8D)
- **Backgrounds**: Alice Blue (#F0F8FF) for soft sections

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: DM Sans (modern, clean, professional)
- **Secondary Font**: Rethink Sans (bold, contemporary)
- **Font Weight**: Regular 400 to Bold 800
- **Style**: Modern, minimal, corporate but approachable

### Design Style
- **Overall Aesthetic**: Modern B2B SaaS / FinTech
- **Approach**: Professional yet approachable, tech-forward, trustworthy
- **Visual Language**: Clean, minimalist, with glassmorphism effects
- **Mood**: Confident, innovative, growth-oriented, secure

## Summary of 3D Image Requirements

### Key Design Principles for ALL Images:
1. **Color Consistency**: Deep Navy (#0B1F3A) + Teal (#42BDBD) always
2. **Style**: Modern, professional 3D render (not cartoon, not hyper-realistic)
3. **Mood**: Professional, trustworthy, innovative, growth-oriented
4. **Aesthetic**: B2B SaaS / FinTech contemporary style
5. **Typography-inspired**: Clean, minimalist, modern geometry (inspired by DM Sans + Rethink Sans fonts)

### Technical Specifications:
- **Rendering**: Medium to high polish 3D (contemporary business photography style)
- **Resolution**: High resolution suitable for web (3840x2160 or higher)
- **Format**: PNG or JPG, optimized for web
- **Color Space**: sRGB for consistent web display
- **Aspect Ratios**: 16:9 for heroes/banners, variable for cards

### Tone & Voice in Visuals:
- Professional yet approachable
- Modern and innovative
- Trustworthy and competent
- Inclusive and diverse
- Growth-focused and optimistic
- Clean and organized
